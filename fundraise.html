This page is for people in the United States who want to raise money for Sadhana where they live. Here are some ideas, and how to get started.

Ideas to get you started

  • School clubs and student groups

    Bake sales, spirit days

  • Cultural and community associations

    Events, food stalls

  • Temples and community centers

    A collection at a festival or gathering

  • Local restaurants and shops

    A share-of-sales night, with the owner's permission

  • Garage, book, or plant sales

  • Walkathons, sports tournaments, or talent shows

How it works

Tell us your idea using the form below. A founder will reply with next steps. Money raised is handled the same way as donations: a founder records it and we pass the full amount to Sadhana.

Good to know

  • Get permission from your school, venue, or organization first.
  • Some activities, such as raffles and public collections, have local rules, so check before you start.
  • We are a student-run group and not a registered charity, so we cannot offer tax receipts.
  • This site does not take payments.
