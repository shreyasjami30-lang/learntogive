Why tutors matter

Every class is taught by volunteer tutors, and nobody is paid. Tutors are the reason our classes can stay free for families.

  • Subjects you can teach

    • Teaching languages

      Every class has a Telugu version and an English version. You can teach in Telugu, English, or both.

    • The commitment

      • Once a week, for the full year.
      • One hour of teaching each week, plus time to prepare.

    • Preparation

      Before each class, review the problems in the curriculum packet so you can explain them clearly and answer questions.

    • Requirements

      Comfortable teaching the subject, able to commit weekly for the full year, and reliable about showing up.

    • Keeping students safe

      • Two tutors teach together in every session.
      • Tutors do not contact students privately and use only approved group channels.
