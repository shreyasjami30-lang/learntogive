About Learn To Give

Learn To Give is a volunteer tutoring group run by three high school students in Georgia, USA. We teach 4th and 5th graders in Hyderabad and Vijayawada, India, online over Zoom.

Our classes are free, our tutors are student volunteers, and nobody is paid. Every donation we receive goes to Sadhana Society.

Meet the Founders

The founder bios and our story are only in English for now.

    Why we started this

    Two of us are daughters of immigrants with roots in Hyderabad and Vijayawada. Our families moved for better opportunities, and we grew up seeing how much education shaped what was possible for us. We also know that many kids back home have no one to help with math or science once the school day ends.

    In July 2025 we started Learn To Give to offer free tutoring to 4th and 5th graders in the region we come from. We are aware that opportunities can be hard to find, and we wanted to help make them easier to reach. Since then we've taught more than 50 students in Telugu and English, with volunteer tutors who give their time for free. Every donation we receive goes to Sadhana Society, so the learning our students get also helps children who need specialized care.
