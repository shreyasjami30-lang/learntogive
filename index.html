What we do

Our volunteer tutors teach math, science, and Intro to Python to 4th and 5th graders over Zoom. Each class meets once a week for an hour, and families choose a Telugu or English version.

  • Math

    4th and 5th grade math, practiced step by step.

  • Science

    4th and 5th grade science about how the world works.

  • Intro to Python

    A first, friendly step into coding.
