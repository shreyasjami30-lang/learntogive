Class details

    How classes work

    • Classes happen live on Zoom, one hour per week, as a year-long program.
    • Classes are year-long and follow the Indian school year, from June to April.
    • Students can join at any time and follow along from wherever the class currently is.
    • At sign-up, families choose English or Telugu. Every class has both versions, and each version has its own meeting time. Every tutor speaks both languages.
    • Each student gets their own curriculum packet, and tutors go over its problems in class.
    • There is homework every week. It usually takes about 2 to 3 hours a week, depending on the student and the rigor of the class.
    • Two volunteer tutors are present in every session.
    • Tuition is free. Donations are optional.
    • A parent or guardian fills out the sign-up form and gives consent. Our team processes the sign-up, then adds your family to our Discord server.
    • All communication with students, parents, and tutors happens on our Discord server. Families get access after their sign-up is processed.

    How a class looks

    Every class is a live Zoom call. Two volunteer tutors share a page from the curriculum packet on screen and work through the problems together with the students.
