Our classes are always free. Donations are optional and support Sadhana Society.

How donating works

  1. You contact our team.

  2. A founder sends you bank-transfer instructions privately.

  3. The founder records your donation.

  4. Once a meaningful amount has built up, we send the full donation amount to Sadhana.

About Sadhana

Sadhana Society for the Mentally Handicapped

Sadhana serves children with intellectual and developmental disabilities through a residential home and training center. Their funding goes toward specialized medical care, physical therapy equipment, and self-help training tools.

A friendly note on transparency

We are a student-run group, not a registered charity. That means we cannot offer tax receipts, and we do not make any claims that donations are tax-deductible. Every donation we receive goes to Sadhana.
